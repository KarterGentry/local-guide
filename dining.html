Beach House

Beach House Bar & Grille, located at Wright Park Dr in Dunkirk, NY, offers a unique and welcoming experience with its combination of great food, friendly service, and a relaxed atmosphere. Nestled by the lake, the restaurant offers a picturesque setting, although patrons may want to be mindful of the lake flies, which can sometimes be a nuisance during warmer months. Despite this, the location is a great spot for enjoying a meal with loved ones or unwinding after a long day.

Mary's Deli

Mary's Deli started as a local grocery store run by Rose and Samuel Lima, parents of Mary Russo. With a drive for success and a dream in mind, Mary transformed her parent's grocery store into the pizzeria it is now in 1979.

P*Dubs / Pizza Wings N' Things

Pizza Wings N' Things first opened at 36 West Main Street in Fredonia in 1978 and was destroyed by a fire later that same year. It reopened in 1979 after extreme perseverance and dedication prevailed. The second location opened at 404 Central Avenue in Dunkirk in 2001. Always known for great pizza and wings, Pizza Wings N’ Things is most famous for its Chicken Finger subs, which were originally thought up by the Murphy boys back in the early 1980s. The Chicken Finger sub sensation grew and now they can be found in sub shops all over the country…but none compare to the original! Our Pizza recipe is the same as it was back in the 1970s and only calls for the best sauce ingredients as well as 100% real mozzarella and lots of it!
